Research

My research explores galaxy evolution, with a focus on the circumgalactic medium, galactic winds, and numerical simulations.

Research Themes

Circumgalactic Medium (CGM)

The structure, thermodynamics, and evolution of diffuse halo gas around galaxies.

Galactic Winds

How stellar feedback drives outflows, shapes galaxy growth, and redistributes mass, metals, and energy.

Numerical Simulations

High-resolution simulations of multiphase gas, cooling, conduction, turbulence, and feedback processes.

Analytical Models

Development of analytical frameworks to understand and predict the behavior of galactic and circumgalactic gas.
