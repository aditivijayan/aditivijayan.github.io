Research Activities

Supernova activity in a galaxy launches powerful galactic winds. These winds transport mass, energy, momentum, and metals from the galaxy to its surrounding circumgalactic medium (CGM).

I study the physics of these exchanges and their observational signatures using a combination of techiques.

Numerical Simulations

High-resolution simulations of supernova launch, galactic outflows, cooling, and evolution of multiphase gas.

Analytical Models

Development of analytical frameworks to understand and predict the behavior of galactic and circumgalactic gas.

Mock Observations

Making synthetic observations to test and validate theoretical models.
