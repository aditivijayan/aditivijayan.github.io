I am a Research Scientist at the Arizona State University (ASU).

I use sophisticated numerical simulations and analytical models to study the physics of galaxy evolution, with a focus on the circumgalactic medium, galactic winds, and feedback processes.

I graduated with a PhD in Astronomy from the Indian Institute of Science in 2020 as Joint Astronomy Programme student and worked at the Australian National University as a PostDoctoral Fellow before moving to ASU.
